/// ETYMOLOGY

WHAT DOES IT MEAN?

Krishi (कृषि): Agriculture & Farming

Setu (सेतु): Bridge

Krishi Setu is the technological bridge connecting the world's most advanced Artificial Intelligence directly to the deeply disconnected, internet-deprived rural farms of India.

PROBLEM STATEMENT 4
THEME: COOPERATION

The Problem

Small and marginal farmers across India lack access to data-driven agricultural guidance. Relying on traditional methods instead of satellite data, soil health analytics, and climate forecasting leads to crop failure and threatens food security. The absence of shared digital infrastructure also blocks cross-state collaboration on climate-resilient farming.

The Challenge

Build an interoperable digital agriculture network that delivers real-time, localised agro-advisories using AI. It should offer regenerative crop recommendations based on satellite data, soil health, and weather forecasting, plus a diagnostic tool for crop diseases, and be designed as a scalable digital public good enabling Indian states to share agricultural data models and strengthen cooperation on sustainable food production.

/// THE CRISIS

WHY KRISHI SETU?

THE CONNECTIVITY GAP

Most cutting-edge agricultural AI tools fail at the last mile. Why? Because they require high-speed internet. In rural India, farm fields are dead zones. Farmers cannot upload 4K photos to the cloud to get help when their crops are dying.

THE LANGUAGE BARRIER

Even when AI tools work, they speak English. We asked: What if an AI could not only diagnose a crop instantly without the internet, but speak the remedy out loud in native Odia and Hindi?

/// THE SOLUTION

CORE SYSTEMS

🔌

100% OFFLINE AI

Downloads lightweight TensorFlow.js models directly into the device's IndexedDB. Point the camera, get a diagnosis. Zero data required.

🌍

NATIVE TONGUE

Speaks the farmer's language. Real-time diagnostic translations in English, Odia, and Hindi, featuring built-in voice readouts.

/// THE ARCHITECTURE

TECH STACK

TENSORFLOW.JS

Executes quantized machine learning vision models directly on the farmer's mobile CPU/GPU. The models are permanently cached inside the browser's IndexedDB, allowing for instantaneous, zero-latency inference without ever sending photos to a server.

REACT + VITE (PWA)

The foundational frontend framework. It utilizes aggressive Service Worker caching strategies to intercept network requests, transforming the website into an installable Progressive Web App that survives in 100% offline environments.

GEMINI 3.6 FLASH

Powers the translation and generative agronomy advisory engine. When a connection is available, Gemini translates complex chemical and organic remedies into localized, easy-to-understand native dialects (Odia & Hindi).

OPENSTREETMAP

Provides the mapping layer for the DPI (Digital Public Infrastructure) Grid. Integrated via Leaflet.js, it broadcasts localized disease outbreaks to neighboring districts to prevent widespread crop failure.

/// KNOWLEDGE BASE

F.A.Q.

How does it scan crops without the internet?

The magic is in the browser. When the app is launched for the first time, a Service Worker downloads the core app UI and the actual AI Model into the device's local storage. From then on, the device's own hardware runs the calculations.

Is the farmer's data private?

100%. Because inference happens locally on the edge via TensorFlow.js, pictures of the farmer's crops never leave their phone. The only data transmitted is an anonymous disease alert pinged to the DPI Grid (if online).

Which languages are supported?

Currently, the UI and the AI advisory system natively support English, Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ), and Hindi (हिन्दी). It also utilizes the Web Speech API to read the remedies out loud to farmers who may struggle with reading.

Do they need to download an app from the Play Store?

No. Krishi Setu is a PWA (Progressive Web App). By simply visiting the URL, their browser will prompt them to "Add to Home Screen". It bypasses app store friction entirely while behaving exactly like a native app.
