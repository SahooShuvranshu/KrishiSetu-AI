SLIDE 5: MACHINE LEARNING & EDGE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title: Machine Learning & Edge Architecture
Subtitle: Bringing state-of-the-art computer vision to low-end Android hardware.

Technical Specifications:
• Neural Architecture: MobileNetV2 fine-tuned with transfer learning for agricultural disease classes across Paddy, Cotton, Tomato, Potato, and Maize.
• Quantization & Size: Model quantized to lightweight TF.js shards (~4.6 MB total footprint) that load in under 2 seconds.
• Private Device Storage: Model weights live in the browser's Origin Private File System (OPFS) — immune to cache purges and surviving device reboots.
• Dual Engine Pipeline: Client-side TF.js for guaranteed offline execution; optional Google Gemini Flash for cloud multimodal reasoning when online.

SLIDE 7: SAFETY-FIRST ENGINEERING — KNOWING WHEN NOT TO ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title: Safety-First Engineering: Knowing When NOT to Answer
Subtitle: In agriculture, an incorrect chemical dosage is far worse than no answer.

Four Protective Guardrails:
1. Dynamic Crop Masking: Renormalizes predictions strictly within the selected crop. A paddy leaf cannot misclassify as tomato blight.
2. Confidence & Margin Gates: Requires ≥50% confidence AND a ≥12% margin over the second candidate; otherwise surfaces "Uncertain — Verify with Officer".
3. Non-Leaf Pixel Guard: Analyzes green spectrum and luminance variance before running inference, rejecting hands, walls, and soil in under 5ms.
4. Trained Negative Class: Includes explicit 'Other_NotALeaf' training samples to eliminate false positive diagnoses.
